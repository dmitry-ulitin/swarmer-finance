Swarmer Finance — Terms of Service

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Swarmer Finance is a private personal-finance application used by one family. It is not offered to the public, and accounts are created only for people the owner has invited.

Use

The application is provided free of charge for keeping track of its users' own income, expenses and transfers. Users are responsible for the data they enter or import and for keeping their password secret.

No financial advice

Balances, exchange rates and summaries are shown for information only. Exchange rates come from third-party sources and may be missing or out of date. Nothing in the application is financial, tax or legal advice.

No warranty

The application is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. It may be unavailable, change or stop at any time. Backups are taken, but the owner is not liable for any loss of data or for any decision made on the basis of what the application shows.

Google Drive access

The Google OAuth client "swarmer-backup" is used only by the application's owner to store encrypted database backups in the owner's own Google Drive, as described in the Privacy Policy.

Ending use

A user may stop using the application at any time and ask for their data to be deleted. The owner may close an account or the application itself.
