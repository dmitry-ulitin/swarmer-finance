Swarmer Finance — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Swarmer Finance is a private personal-finance application used by one family. It is not offered to the public.

Data the application stores

Accounts, categories and transactions that its users enter or import, and their email address and a hashed password for signing in. This data is stored in the application's own database and is visible only to the users an account has been shared with.

Google Drive access

The Google OAuth client "swarmer-backup" is used only by the application's owner to upload encrypted backups of the application's database to the owner's own Google Drive. It requests the drive.file scope, so it can see and change only the files it has created itself, and no other files in the Drive. It does not read, share or transfer any other Google user data.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
